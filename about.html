Our story

From a Surulere kitchen to Lekki’s favourite Nigerian restaurant.

2014 to today

Mama’s pot, proper standards

Chef Adaeze Okafor began cooking Sunday jollof for neighbours. Orders grew, a shop followed, and in 2018 the Lekki dining room opened. Today we cook Yoruba amala, Igbo oha, Efik afang, Hausa suya and Delta banga from family recipes.

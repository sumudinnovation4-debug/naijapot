Chef’s table

Chef Adaeze Okafor

Adaeze started cooking Sunday jollof for her neighbours in Surulere in 2014. She now runs a brigade of 18 cooks who prep every morning at 6am. Peppers are blended fresh, stocks are made from bones, and nothing is microwaved.

“Nigerian food does not need to be fancied up. It needs respect, time and heat.”
